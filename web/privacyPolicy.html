Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Thank you for using Podo Korean ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Email address (when signing in via Google, Apple, or email login)
  • Usage data such as lesson progress and activity history
  • Crash logs and device information (for performance improvements)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To personalize your learning experience
  • To sync your progress across devices
  • To improve the performance and features of the app
  • To contact you for important updates (if you opt in)

3. Third-Party Services

We may share limited data with the following third-party services for app functionality and analytics:

  • Firebase – Authentication, Analytics, Crash Reporting
  • RevenueCat – Subscription and purchase management
  • Zoho – Email communication (only if you opt in)

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain user data for as long as your account is active. You have full control over your data and can delete your account at any time.

  • Manual Deletion: To delete your account yourself, go to the My Page menu in the app and tap Remove Account. This will permanently erase your account and all associated data.
  • Automatic Deletion Due to Inactivity: If you do not log in for over one year, you will receive an email notification about the upcoming account deletion. After the notification is sent, if you still do not log in within 7 days, your account will be considered inactive and will be automatically deleted.

You may also request deletion by contacting us directly at contact@podokorean.com.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal information. You can manage your preferences from within the app or by contacting us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: contact@podokorean.com
